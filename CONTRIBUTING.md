# HELPIM — ritim dispela pastaim long yu stretim samting

*Tok Pisin. Tanim tok bilong Tanakh bilong Selah (selah-tpi).*

Mipela laikim ai bilong yu. Tasol **sampela samting hia i luk olsem rong, tasol mipela i mekim long laik**, na sapos
yu senisim, bai yu bagarapim as bilong ol dispela tok. Olsem na ritim namba wan hap pastaim.

---

## I. Ol samting yu no ken stretim — mipela i makim, i no rong

### `Yahwe`

`יהוה` i raitim **`Yahwe`**, i no *Yahweh*, i no *Jehova*. **No ken senisim `Yahwe` i go long `Bikpela`, `BIKPELA`,
`Masta`, `Jehova`, `Papa God`, o `God`.** Em i as bilong olgeta dispela tok: rida i mas lukim Nem olsem em i stap
long Hibru.

### `Elohim`, `El`, `Adonai`, `Sadai`

`אלהים` em **`Elohim`**; `אל` em **`El`**; `אדני` bilong God em **`Adonai`**; `שדי` em **`Sadai`**;
`יהוה צבאות` em **`Yahwe Tsebaot`** — i no gat narapela tok namel.

### `bikman`, `masta bilong mi`, `god` long liklik leta

- **`bikman` / `masta bilong mi`** long wanpela man (`אדון`, `אדני` taim em i toktok long man) — orait.
- **`god` / `ol god`** long ol god bilong ol lain na ol piksa — orait.

Rong em i taim *Bikpela* o *God* i sanap long ples bilong `יהוה` o `אלהים`.

### Ol tok i stap long nois bilong Hibru

**`ruak`** (`רוח` — spirit, win na pulim win long wanpela tok), **`khesed`**, **`Tora`**, **`Sabat`**. Ol dispela i no
rong.

### `profet` i no `glasman`

`נביא` em **`profet`**. *Glasman* na *posin man* i stap tasol taim Hibru i toktok long ol glasman (Lo 18:10, 14).

### `⟨את⟩` na `⟨ ⟩`

`⟨את⟩` em mak bilong Hibru, i stap long laik. Ol tok Tok Pisin insait long `⟨ ⟩` em ol tok Tok Pisin i mas gat na
Hibru i no raitim. **Insait long `⟨ ⟩` i no gat Inglis** (*the*, *of*).

### Stat 1:26 — planti i stap planti

`נעשה אדם בצלמנו` — *yumi wokim man long piksa bilong yumi*. Planti bilong Hibru i no senis.

---

## II. Ol samting i mas stretim — hia mipela i nidim yu

- **Tok Pisin i no pasin bilong toktok** — tok i stret tasol ol man i no save tok olsem.
- **Inglis, Bislama (*blong*, *hemi*) o Solomon Pijin (*mifala*, *yufala*)** i stap yet long ves.
- **Ves (`translation`) na ol tok (`tokens`) i no wankain** — ol tok i bosim; ves i bihainim.
- **Ol askim long `NOTES.md`** — ol i bikpela samting.

## III. Olsem wanem long stretim

1. Opim fail bilong ves (`<buk>/<sapta>/<ves>.json`).
2. Stretim `translation` o `gloss` — **no ken tasim `surface`** (ol leta bilong Hibru).
3. Long tok bilong commit, raitim ves, wanem samting yu senisim, na as bilong en.
4. Sapos yu no save tru, raitim long `NOTES.md` — no ken senisim.

Tenkyu long helpim.
